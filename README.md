## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Ghostfolio really free?
Yes, completely free. No hidden fees, no premium tiers. Just open-source software maintained by a community of developers.

### Do I need an internet connection to use it?
Ghostfolio works offline, but needs internet to fetch current market prices. Once prices are stored, the app functions entirely offline.

### Can I track multiple accounts?
Absolutely. Create separate categories for your brokerage accounts, retirement funds, or crypto wallets. Ghostfolio keeps them organized.

### What if I make a mistake entering a transaction?
Simply locate the transaction in your history and click "Edit." You can correct any entry — shares, price, or date.

### How often are prices updated?
Ghostfolio updates prices every 5 minutes during market hours. For cryptocurrencies, updates occur continuously.

## 🔧 Troubleshooting Common Issues

### Ghostfolio Won't Start
- Close other programs that use port 3000 (like other development tools)
- Restart your computer and try again
- Check if your antivirus blocked the installation (temporarily disable, then re-enable after running)

### Dashboard Shows "Connection Error"
- Ensure your internet is active
- Refresh the page after 30 seconds
- Check if your firewall blocks localhost connections

### Can't Find My Cryptocurrency
- Try the full name (e.g., "Ethereum" instead of "ETH")
- Verify the ticker symbol with the currency's official listing
